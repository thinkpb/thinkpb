TrainCue Support

TrainCue is a workout tracking and training guidance app designed to help users follow structured training plans, log workouts, and review progress over time.

Contact Support

For help, bug reports, or privacy requests, contact:

support@traincue.app

What to Include

When contacting support, please include your device model, iOS version, app version, a short description of the issue, and screenshots if helpful.

Medical Disclaimer

TrainCue is not a medical app and does not provide medical advice. Consult a qualified professional before starting or changing an exercise program.
