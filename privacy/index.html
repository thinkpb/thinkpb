TrainCue Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 25, 2026

TrainCue is a workout tracking and training guidance app that helps users create, manage, and review workout programs.

Information We Collect

TrainCue may collect or process information that you choose to enter into the app, including workout plans, exercises, sets, reps, weights, notes, training history, program preferences, and fitness goals.

AI-Assisted Guidance

If you use AI-assisted features, workout-related inputs may be processed to generate coaching suggestions, program adjustments, or training feedback. Do not enter sensitive medical information, diagnoses, or information you do not want processed for app functionality.

How We Use Information

We use workout and app information to provide workout tracking features, display training history, generate AI-assisted training guidance, improve app functionality, and respond to support requests.

Health and Medical Disclaimer

TrainCue is not a medical app and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. The app is intended for general fitness tracking and training organization. Consult a qualified healthcare or fitness professional before starting or changing an exercise program.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with service providers only as needed to operate app functionality, such as cloud hosting, analytics, crash reporting, or AI processing, depending on the services enabled in the app.

Tracking and Advertising

TrainCue does not use workout or fitness data for third-party advertising or behavioral advertising.

Data Retention

Workout data is retained as needed to provide app functionality. If data is stored locally on your device, deleting the app may delete locally stored data. If cloud features are added, additional retention and deletion controls may apply.

Children

TrainCue is not intended for children under 13.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed by the app. No system is completely secure.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact: support@traincue.app
